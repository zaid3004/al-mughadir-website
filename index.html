About the shop

Your neighbourhood electronics counter in Rolla.

Al Mughadir sits inside Rolla Mall on Khalid Bin Al Waleed Street, a short walk from Hamza Bin Abdulmuttalib Mosque and Rolla Textile Souq. The counter stocks phones, watches, computers and accessories, with a repair bench on hand for quick fixes.
